# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Fitroom to be a place where people can ask questions, share work, and disagree about
code without being treated badly. We welcome contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work and kind to the person.
- Give reasons for review comments. Point to code, tests, or cited sources.
- Assume good intent, then ask when something is unclear.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to keep the project small or defer a feature.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Bad faith arguments about a person's identity or right to take part.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space. It
also applies when you represent Fitroom in public.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer listed in `CITATION.cff`. For security reports,
use the private route in `SECURITY.md` instead.

Include what happened, where it happened, and any links or screenshots that help. The maintainer
will keep the report private to the extent the law allows.

## Enforcement

The maintainer will review the report, talk to the people involved when needed, and choose a
response. Responses range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
The maintainer may remove content that breaks this policy.

Retaliation against a person who reports a problem is not allowed.
